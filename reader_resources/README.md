# Appendix B decision workbooks

[Home](../README.md) · [Solutions and appendices](../navigation/SOLUTIONS.md)

Use these optional templates with Appendix B, *From AI Claims to Engineering Decisions*, to turn a research claim into a local engineering question and an evidence-collection plan.

| Format | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Text workbook](Appendix_B_Decision_Workbook.txt) | Read and fill in a human-readable worksheet. |
| [JSON workbook](Appendix_B_Decision_Workbook.json) | Record the structured version for processing with your own tools. |

Choose the format that fits your workflow; you do not need to complete both. Copy the template to your own working location before filling it in. Keep illustrative values distinct from actual observations and leave unknown results unknown. No extra software or model account is required to read these files.
