ILLUSTRATIVE TREASURY PLANNING EXAMPLE
Status: PLAN ONLY. No new model trial or human pilot is reported.

Question: Can agent-drafted supplementary tests expose an agreed v1 routing
fault, and how much effort would preparing and reviewing them require?
Baseline: The team's current test preparation and review under the same
approved policy and acceptance obligations.
Change: The agent drafts supplementary tests; the reviewer retains control of
policy-derived acceptance expectations and the acceptance decision.
Scope: BR-001/v1, EUR 5000 boundary. The later v2 policy is a separate contract.

Source: Zhao, Zhou and Cohen, Evaluating and Mitigating the Misguidance Effect
of Buggy Code in LLM-Generated Unit Tests, arXiv 2607.22883v1.
URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.22883v1
Atlas: W077. Inspect Sections 2.2 and 2.5 and Table 2.
Finding: Buggy code can lead generated tests to reinforce faulty behavior.
The paper's mitigation uses a model-generated docstring derived from code.
Local adaptation: Use the approved Treasury policy to supply the expected
answer. This independent policy input is not the paper's tested intervention.

Proposed challenge: A hypothetical faulty comparison sends exactly EUR 5000
to the standard queue. The v1 policy requires human review. Check that the
intended assertion fails on that faulty behavior and passes after the repair;
retain nearby valid and invalid cases. An import error is a setup failure,
not detection of the boundary defect.

Test interpretation:
  Faulty FAIL, repaired PASS: distinguishes this defect.
  Faulty PASS, repaired PASS: does not expose this defect; may cover other work.
  Faulty PASS, repaired FAIL: may favor the faulty behavior.
  Both FAIL: investigate assertion, candidate and setup.

Lab connection: From companion/, run python3 reader_start.py show 9 to rehearse
Chapter 9's evidence mechanism. CP09 permits candidate.py and review.json.
Its gate does not assess this added test-generation comparison. Create that
comparison in a separately declared evaluation directory, leaving protected
companion material intact. Prior exposure to Appendix A must be recorded.

Proposed next action: Prepare the bounded comparison with review retained.
Actual technical results, human effort, accepted outcomes and charges: UNKNOWN.
Stop or narrow if behavior is disputed, protected expectations change, evidence
is stale or an action exceeds its permissions. Reconsider after relevant model,
harness, context, policy or task-population changes.
