How to read the four volumes

Begin with the engineering question you need to answer. Each volume stands on its own; the references let you deepen a decision without reading the whole series first.

Your question Start here Then use
How do I conduct a change with an agent and judge its result? Engineering Software with Agentic AI, Chapters 1–12 and the eight missions Its recorded sessions, work orders and independent checks.
Which practices deserve trust, and what are their limits? Non-Deterministic Software Engineering, second edition Its research synthesis and broader treatment of security, memory, learning and organizations.
How do I turn intent into specifications, gates and evidence a framework can enforce, and when is that process worth its cost? Non-Deterministic Spec-Driven Development, second edition Its comparison of public frameworks, pattern catalogue, playbooks and templates.
How do I build the execution system around an agent? Loop Engineering for AI Agents Its harness, state, budget, stopping and installation recipes.

Follow the next unresolved question

  • Authorized behavior: In §§4.1–4.5, Non-Deterministic Software Engineering separates intention from implementation and derives behavioral examples. The work order in Engineering Software with Agentic AI cites that authority in Expect, then adds delegation and checking.
  • Execution: Loop §4.5 develops an external CLI controller; §§5.6–5.7 cover reproducible installation and a controlled local runtime. Recheck their dated environment and the current companion’s client-verification status before use.
  • Context, memory and security: Chapter 4 of Engineering Software with Agentic AI develops context selection, memory promotion and retrieval, contradiction handling, forgetting, and tool enforcement. Its optional B1 memory lab exercises these record controls. Chapter 9 of Non-Deterministic Software Engineering supplies the program-comprehension and persistent-memory research; Chapter 6 develops product and agent-runtime threat models.
  • Acceptance: §§7.7–7.9 and §§8.8–8.10 of Non-Deterministic Software Engineering develop independent checking, risk-based review and accountable verdicts. Loop §6.4.2 develops maker–checker separation. Chapters 8–9 of Engineering Software with Agentic AI put these decisions into candidate, integration and artifact checks.
  • Specification and gates: Non-Deterministic Spec-Driven Development §§2.6–2.8 and §6.5 develop clarification, consistency analysis and specification checklists; §12.9 defines its gates, and §§14.4–14.8 its autonomous cycles, budgets, evidence packs, escalations and convergence review. Chapters 3, 6 and 8–9 of Engineering Software with Agentic AI decide whether an identified candidate satisfies the resulting contract.
  • Learning and adoption: Chapter 12 of Engineering Software with Agentic AI develops prediction, assisted practice, reduced support, delayed retention, transfer and calibration, then evaluates adoption and its whole cost. Chapter 10 of Non-Deterministic Software Engineering supplies the research and capability-development framework; its Chapters 11–12 cover team governance and economics. Sections 13.1–13.4 help select the smallest adequate practice. A memory update and demonstrated human learning remain separate outcomes. Non-Deterministic Spec-Driven Development Chapter 16 states when a specification process costs more than the failures it prevents.

Keep the contracts distinct

The books use different Treasury teaching applications. In Non-Deterministic Software Engineering, the domestic, low-risk v1 eligibility ceiling includes EUR 5,000. BR-001/v1 in Engineering Software with Agentic AI sends equality to human review and classifies queues; it does not authorize payment. Keep each rule, unit and expected answer together.

An intention contract authorizes behavior; an EDOD work order organizes its execution and acceptance; a task envelope helps choose scope and controls. Permissions still need enforcement in the actual runtime. The gate verdicts, evidence packs and escalation tiers of Non-Deterministic Spec-Driven Development describe the framework in that book; a passed gate or an agent’s self-assessed verdict does not replace the independent check and the owner’s decision. The book’s glossary crosswalks preserve these distinctions.
