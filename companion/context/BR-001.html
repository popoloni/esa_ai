BR-001/v1 — routing policy

Status: approved. Owner: Treasury policy owner. Fictional case effective date: 2025-05-12.

Reject an amount that cannot be parsed as a Decimal, is not finite, or is at most zero. Route a finite positive amount below EUR 5,000 to STANDARD_QUEUE / BELOW_LIMIT. Route EUR 5,000 or more to HUMAN_REVIEW / AT_OR_ABOVE_LIMIT. A rejection is REJECTED / INVALID_AMOUNT. Return policy_version BR-001/v1. These routes classify work; they do not authorize a payment. The rule does not settle a narrower textual grammar: exponent and underscore restrictions need an owner decision.
