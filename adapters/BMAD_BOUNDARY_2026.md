# BMad planning comparison — documentation snapshot, 19 October 2026

Official: https://docs.bmad-method.org/plan/plan-inside-an-organization/ and https://docs.bmad-method.org/plan/define-requirements-and-a-specification/

For an organizational project, BMad treats PRD and sign-off as multi-person decisions; for smaller work it explicitly permits lightweight issue/spec paths. Do not impose a universal PRD on the bounded alpha; the product owner may choose the smallest adequate document after DISC-003/v1. Tool commands are not executable in this offline CP04 lab.
