# Portable architecture exploration prompt (author-created teaching example)

Task: compare `direct` and `ports` prototype designs against the reviewed
SPEC-004/v1; do not change the policy, business scope or reference oracle.

Return: (1) constraints; (2) how both implement all eight selected cases;
(3) interfaces and dependency direction; (4) consequences of a second provider;
(5) complexity cost; (6) observability and security unknowns;
(7) a proposed ADR including reasons, reversibility and revisit triggers;
(8) evidence not yet available. Do not fabricate latency, productivity, or
human comprehension measurements. Do not mark a release as approved.

An agent may propose the ADR. A human technical owner must decide.
