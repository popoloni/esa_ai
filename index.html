Continue from your own work

Keep the complete candidate directory, including READER_ENTRY.json and its receipts, when switching editions. A retained candidate from companion 5.3, 5.4 or 5.5 can continue in this edition. Use advance after a successful handoff. It checks and carries the retained predecessor into a fresh candidate. Use new only for an independent entry. Keep failed attempts and choose new receipt filenames.

python3 companion/start.py advance G2 --from-candidate work/g1 --dest work/g2

B1 has a separate v2 extension before B2. G3 has a report/CLI integration step before G4. Follow the complete mission sequence and the integration guide.
