Reference implementations and proposal review

Preserve an implementation attempt and your written proposal decisions before opening the references. These are supplied teaching solutions, separate from your candidate and from actual agent recordings.

Export a reference to its own folder and identify its checks explicitly:

python3 companion/start.py solution B3 --dest work/solution-b3
python3 companion/start.py check B3 --candidate work/solution-b3 --reference --output work/evidence/b3-reference.json

A reference cannot become a learner handoff. Read the proposal-review guide before revealing TRUTH.json or a batch review receipt.
