# Treasury team operating agreement — filled practice example

Version: TEAM-TREASURY/1. Status: PROPOSED TABLETOP AGREEMENT. The roles, queue events and limits below are fictional teaching choices, not records of a team meeting or real approval.

| Decision | Worked agreement |
|---|---|
| Purpose and boundary | Maintain an inspectable routing explanation and incident handoff. Reduced reviewer search effort is a hypothesis to measure; payment authority is excluded. |
| Work admitted | One chapter-scoped change at a time per maker. Each item names its input snapshot, permitted files, expected refusal and accountable reviewer. |
| Roles | The product/policy owner settles intended behavior; maker proposes changes; technical reviewer challenges evidence; operator owns any real rollout; knowledge steward maintains approved instructions. Roles may combine, but the maker cannot create an independent owner decision. |
| Agent use | Read approved source, index evidence and draft a candidate or review question within the assigned boundary. Protected oracle, owner records and secrets remain outside its write authority. Actual runtime restrictions need their own configuration evidence. |
| Common versus personal tools | Shared contracts, required checks, review records and context versions are team-owned. Personal editor choices are allowed only when they preserve those interfaces and data constraints. No live-client compatibility has been established by this sheet. |
| Data and integrations | Synthetic Treasury material only in this practice. A real tool/data or permission change waits for its relevant owner. No production credentials or customer records are introduced. |
| Review capacity | Illustrative limits: two active implementation items and one item waiting for technical review. A full review queue stops new implementation starts; help complete or reduce existing work. Review capacity includes incidents and mentoring. |
| Stop and disagreement | Stop for changed scope, missing policy authority, altered protected expectations, unexpected access or missing recovery evidence. Record the unresolved question and owner. The lead resolves prioritization, not another owner's missing authorization. |
| Context maintenance | Knowledge steward reviews changed prerequisites and evidence links before publishing a revision. Keep the earlier version, change reason and decision; retire instructions that cannot meet current conditions. |
| Measures and next review | At the next agreed team review, examine queue age, review effort and missingness, confirmed defects, unresolved decisions and estimated versus observed cost. Use Chapter 14 metric cards. No success rate or savings is prefilled. |
| Outstanding obligations | T07-03 remains pending and T07-04 blocked; CP12 reconciliation remains pending. A team agreement does not renew release authority or validate the CP13 rubric. |

## Full-queue tabletop

K1 is ready for technical review, K2 is an active draft and K3 is proposed. The review slot is occupied. The maker of K2 prepares its evidence and helps the reviewer reproduce K1; K3 stays waiting. An incident takes the reviewer away: the lead reduces planned starts or obtains an appropriately qualified reviewer, rather than removing the check. No queue movement is recorded as acceptance.

## Decision after disagreement

The maker proposes promoting an evidence-index skill for all incidents. The reviewer asks whether its state-selection assumptions apply elsewhere. The team keeps the skill proposed, assigns the knowledge steward to prepare a changed-cause example, and reviews that evidence before promotion. This is a worked decision, not an observed experiment result.
