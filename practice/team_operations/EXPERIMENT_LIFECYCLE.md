# From bounded experiment to maintained practice

A trial may end in adoption, revision, rejection or retirement. None is a failure to learn. This worksheet is original process guidance; the filled case below is fictional tabletop reasoning.

## Filled Treasury tabletop

E-TEAM-01 proposes an evidence-index helper for a chapter-scoped review. It may read approved synthetic records and propose links; it may not edit the oracle, source observations or owner decisions. A technical reviewer compares its output with the original records and checks a misleading or missing reference. Human effort and tool charges would be recorded separately. The proposed ceiling is one task and one review session, with numeric cost/time limits chosen before any real run; no run is recorded here.

The reviewer discovers, in this fictional discussion, that the proposed procedure assumes every symptom has the same cause. Decision: revise the procedure and test a changed mechanism before promotion. Until then, the shared default remains unchanged. The example teaches how to withhold adoption; it is not a measured tool result.

For a later proposed adoption, require version/configuration, observed cases and limitations, an accountable maintainer and a fallback. Revisit after a relevant model/tool/permission/schema change or repeated override. If the owner cannot maintain the procedure or its required access is no longer appropriate, retire it and identify the supported replacement. Retain the previous decision and reason; do not erase the failed trial or turn an old approval into perpetual authorization.
