## Review at the declared cutoff

1. Reconcile attempted, accepted, pending, rejected and abandoned work.
2. Show effort/charge coverage and the known subtotal before claiming a complete total.
3. Identify whether each monetary component is observed, allocated or estimated. Actual hours valued at an assumed rate are a modelled cost, not a directly observed wage payment.
4. Compute the complete ratio only with complete in-scope inputs and a known positive denominator. Known zero acceptance gives undefined; absent acceptance evidence gives unknown.
5. Compare actual records with the earlier estimate; preserve both. Explain differences without selecting only successes or claiming causation from a before/after result.
6. Record a bounded continue/change/stop recommendation and the owner decision still required.
