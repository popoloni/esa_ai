# Team operating agreement

Agreement ID/version: ______  Status: PROPOSED / AGREED (link actual decision): ______
Use case and repository/service: ______  Review date or trigger: ______

1. **Outcome and bottleneck.** What should improve, for whom, and what evidence currently supports the problem? What is only a hypothesis? What must not get worse?
2. **Admitted work.** What makes an item ready to start? Identify its input snapshot, permitted changes, selected checks and expected refusal.
3. **Roles and decisions.** Who owns intent, implementation, review, operation and maintained context? Which person can stop work? Which decisions must another owner make?
4. **Agent and data boundaries.** Which tools/actions/data are allowed? Where are permissions actually enforced? What evidence is still missing? What must never be inferred from a prompt or worksheet?
5. **Shared standards and choices.** Which contracts, checks and context are common? Which tool preferences remain personal? Who reviews a new shared dependency or integration?
6. **Capacity.** What is the limit for active implementation and waiting review? How was it chosen? What happens when the queue is full or an incident consumes capacity?
7. **Pairing and onboarding.** Which task will a newcomer observe, explain and then perform under review? Where do questions and first-attempt feedback go?
8. **Maintenance and experiments.** Who versions the shared material? How does a bounded experiment become a reviewed default, change, or retirement?
9. **Measures.** For each selected metric, name unit, denominator/window, source, owner, missingness rule and decision. Keep estimates separate from observations.
10. **Unresolved decisions and next action.** Name the owner, evidence needed and review trigger. A blank or pending item must remain visible.
