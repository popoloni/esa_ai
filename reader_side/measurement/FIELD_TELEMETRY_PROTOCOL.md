# Future Pilot E field collection

This protocol is usable preparation for a future owner-approved pilot. It is
unexecuted. `FIELD_TELEMETRY_TEMPLATE.json` is deliberately unfilled;
`FIELD_TELEMETRY_SCHEMA.json` defines its record structure. These records do not
feed the current automated result or replace the protected synthetic ledger.

1. Copy the template outside protected source. Declare the study ID, small
   matched-work boundary, horizon, nominated owner, currency, permitted tools,
   resource conditions and comparison/control before collecting observations.
   Retain the original protocol and record later deviations separately.
2. Assign a stable logical work-item ID and a separate attempt ID for every
   attempt, including rejection, review, repair, stale evidence and abandonment.
   Bind each to the exact artifact and raw review evidence. A rerun or amended
   artifact is not automatically another work item.
3. Collect actual initial implementation, review and implementation-rework
   hours as mutually exclusive categories. Retain the interval, pseudonymous
   person ID, category, raw record, collection method and resource deviations.
   Self-report remains labeled self-report. Do not count one person's interval
   in two categories; reconcile overlaps before summing. Process elapsed time
   is a separate measurement and cannot supply human effort.
4. Retain provider billing or other cost records, amount, currency, period and
   the explicit allocation rule for shared charges. A telemetry estimate is
   labeled an estimate and is not silently substituted for an invoice. Do not
   sum different currencies. If conversion is necessary, retain the separately
   approved rate/date/source and original amounts before normalized analysis.
5. Ask the authorized owner to record acceptance, rejection or withdrawal for
   the exact logical item, artifact and current review evidence. Preserve the
   raw owner record. Append a new record with `supersedes_record_id` for a later
   decision; never overwrite earlier acceptance or disagreement. Count only
   distinct items whose latest applicable record remains accepted. A stale
   artifact or withdrawn acceptance cannot remain in the denominator.
6. Keep missingness explicit. An unavailable hour, bill or owner record is
   UNKNOWN, not zero. Zero is a positive recorded observation with its evidence.
   Report incomplete coverage before computing a cost-per-accepted-outcome
   field result. A zero accepted denominator is UNDEFINED, not zero cost.
7. Have the owner review exclusions, overlap reconciliation, allocations,
   currency normalization and acceptance applicability. Report small-sample
   variation, setup/rework costs and uncertainty alongside any comparison.
   Preserve all raw records, including unsuccessful attempts. Tool choices
   remain bounded pilot decisions until the owner confirms adequate evidence.

Declared IDs, raw files and local hashes do not authenticate people, prove
independent judgment or supply procurement/release authority. Keep any real
participant or vendor records under the organization's appropriate access
controls. CP13's human/rubric work remains separate and pending; no unfilled
template establishes learning, productivity, ROI or an empirical tool winner.
