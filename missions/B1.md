### Read the grammar as a whole-input contract

Use `[0-9]` and `fullmatch`: `\d` includes Unicode digits, and `$` can match before
a final newline. The checks include non-ASCII text and `"4000\n"` alongside valid
ASCII controls. `isdigit()` is no substitute for the chosen grammar.

B3's HISTORY-API/v1 is different: optional minus, 1–12 integer digits, up to two
fractional digits, leading zeros but no plus. B1 v2 permits either sign and
32 total characters. Thus `+4000.00` is valid routing text but invalid history;
`004000.0` is valid in both and canonicalizes to `4000.00` for history identity.
Do not copy an expression between APIs without its contract.
