The rejection path handles values outside the admitted numeric domain, while the existing result object and policy identity remain unchanged. 5. Expect again: a new decision changes the interface

The second session has the New capability profile. B1-OD-02, the fictional maintainer and rule owner’s decision dated 20 May 2025, introduces B1-API/v2 while retaining BR-001/v1. Create a separate copy of your retained v1 candidate:

python3 companion/start.py extend B1 \
  --candidate work/b1 \

Read work/b1-v2/docs/B1-API-v2.md before its tests. Exact ASCII monetary text now follows [+-]?[0-9]+(?:\.[0-9]{1,2})?, with at most 32 characters including sign and point. Leading zeros and plus signs are allowed. Negative and zero amounts are syntactically recognized but numerically rejected. Nothing is rounded.

Read the grammar as a whole-input contract

Use [0-9] and fullmatch: \d includes Unicode digits, and $ can match before a final newline. The checks include non-ASCII text and "4000\n" alongside valid ASCII controls. isdigit() is no substitute for the chosen grammar.

B3’s HISTORY-API/v1 is different: optional minus, 1–12 integer digits, up to two fractional digits, leading zeros but no plus. B1 v2 permits either sign and 32 total characters. Thus +4000.00 is valid routing text but invalid history; 004000.0 is valid in both and canonicalizes to 4000.00 for history identity. Do not copy an expression between APIs without its contract.
